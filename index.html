Questions Answered

Do participants need an account?
No. Anyone with the meeting link can join directly — no signup, no download, no app required.
How many people can join?
Free rooms support up to 10 participants. Pro rooms scale to 100 with our SFU media cluster.
Is it really end-to-end encrypted?
Yes. WebRTC uses DTLS for key exchange and SRTP for media encryption natively in the browser.
